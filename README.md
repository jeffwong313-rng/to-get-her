# To Get Her

A 2-player co-op, pass-and-play, XCOM-style hex adventure in full 3D anime style. A family, a little sister taken in the darkness, and two heroes who have to bring her back.

- Create two heroes (Sisters, Brothers, Brother and Sister, or Couple).
- Everyone starts at 1 in every SPECIAL stat plus 10 points. No classes: your stats unlock skills.
- Each hero has their own HP, MP and Action Points. AP buys movement and attacks.
- Explore the fog-covered hex map, find camps, "?" tiles, elites and bosses, and carry the materials your enemies drop (stone, wood, hide, scrap metal, ice crystal, carbon, void shards) home to rebuild the town.

## Depth
- Boss rules (Ward, Charger, Relentless, Summoner, Hunter), blessings after elites, and a land twist on each trip from the third trip on.
- Co-op: flanking (+15%), a Bond meter that unlocks Tandem Strike, and Help Up to raise a fallen partner.
- Story beats in each land, synthesized sound and music (no audio files), phone and Z Fold layouts.
- Fatigue (50% tiring, 80% exhausted, 100% injury), camps and Energy Brews recover it. Fainting sends you home without loot.
- Flee: both living heroes must choose Flee (one after the other). Lose 40-70% of gold and materials plus a 1/3 chance per item found this trip; mid-fight multiplies it x2 (x2.5 elite, x3 boss). Gear and levels are kept.
- Appearance editor: hair, face, brows, mouth, shirt, pants, shoes, accessories, each with colours.
- Skills are gated by stat thresholds (3 / 10 / 18 / 26) across Warrior (STR), Archer (AGI), Mage (INT) and Monk (END). Weapons are universal: damage scales with the weapon's stat.
- 47 monsters, each with its own look, signature ability, fighting habit, resistances and skills. Perception scouts them in four stages (look for the 🔍) and fills the Bestiary.
- Press ? for the Guide: definitions for every stat, skill, element and rule. Hover or long-press any underlined term.
- An eight-page storybook opens the game.

## Play
Open the GitHub Pages site on a phone or computer. On a phone, use "Add to Home Screen" to install it.

## Install via GitHub Pages
1. Settings, then Pages.
2. Source: Deploy from a branch, branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`.
3. Visit `https://<your-name>.github.io/to-get-her/`.

No build step: plain scripts plus a bundled three.js.
